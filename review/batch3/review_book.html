Advance Care Planning: Health Care Power of Attorney and Living Will An advance directive is a legal document that lets you state your wishes for medical care if you become unable to make decisions for yourself. A Health Care Power of Attorney names a person (your health care agent) to make medical decisions for you if you cannot. A Living Will states which treatments you would or would not want at the end of life, such as a breathing machine or a feeding tube. These forms must be signed according to your state's rules, which often require witnesses or a notary. Having aphasia does not mean you cannot make your own decisions.
