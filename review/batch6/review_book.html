LOW BLOOD SUGAR (HYPOGLYCEMIA) Low blood sugar is a reading below 70 mg/dL. Signs include shaking, sweating, fast heartbeat, hunger, confusion, and trouble speaking. After a stroke, it can be hard to tell low blood sugar from stroke symptoms, so check your sugar. RULE OF 15: 1. Eat or drink 15 grams of fast sugar: 4 oz (half cup) of juice or regular soda, or 4 glucose tablets. 2. Wait 15 minutes. 3. Check again. 4. If still below 70, repeat. 5. When above 70, eat a snack or meal. If the person cannot swallow safely or is not awake, do not give food or drink. Call 911. Call your diabetes team at (919) 555-0152 if you have low blood sugar more than 2 times in a week.
