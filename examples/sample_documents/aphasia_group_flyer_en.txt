Aphasia Conversation Group. Join other adults living with aphasia for supported conversation, games, and community. The group meets on the first and third Thursday of each month from 1:00 p.m. to 2:30 p.m. at Oakwood Library, 220 Maple Avenue, Room B. Care partners are welcome. There is no cost to attend. To register or ask questions, contact Jordan Ellis, M.S., CCC-SLP, at (919) 555-0158 or jordan.ellis@example.org.
