Aphasia Friendly Docs

Free · English & Spanish

Turn any document into one people with aphasia can read.

This tool turns a document into an aphasia-friendly handout: short sentences, big text, key words in bold, and a picture beside every line.

What is aphasia?

Aphasia is a language disorder. It often happens after a stroke or a brain injury. It can make it hard to speak, understand, read or write. It does not change how smart a person is.

Health letters are often long and full of medical words. Everyone deserves to understand their own care.

How to use it

Three easy steps

  1. Upload

    Choose a PDF, a Word file, or a photo of a health letter.

  2. Pick a language

    English, Spanish, or keep the language of the letter.

  3. Check and print

    Read the handout, fix any words, then download and print it.

What you get

Made for people with aphasia

One idea per line

A picture for each line

Facts are checked

Please note: This tool does not give medical advice. Do not upload real patient information. Always check a handout before giving it to someone, and follow the care team’s instructions.
