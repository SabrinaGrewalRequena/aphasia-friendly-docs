What is aphasia?

Aphasia is a language disorder, often caused by a stroke or brain injury. It can make reading, writing, speaking, and understanding harder, while thinking stays sharp. Clear health information helps people stay safe and in charge of their care.

How to use it

  1. Upload

    Choose a PDF, a Word file, or a photo of a health letter.

  2. Pick a language

    This tool is available in English and Spanish.

  3. Check and print

    Read the handout, fix any words, then download and print it.

What you get

Facts are checked

Please note: This tool does not give medical advice. Do not upload real patient information. Always check a handout before giving it to someone, and follow the care team’s instructions.
