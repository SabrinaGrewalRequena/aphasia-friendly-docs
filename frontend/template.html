Aphasia Handout Maker

Paste a health document. Get an aphasia-friendly handout in English or Spanish: short lines, one topic per page, bold key words, pictures. Every name, date, dose and phone number is checked against the original.

How it works
  1. Claude rewrites the document using the project's aphasia-friendly rules.
  2. The safety check compares every fact with your original.
  3. Click any line on a page to edit it. The check reruns as you type.
  4. Download the handout, open it in your browser, and print it.
